Admission 2026

Four steps to
your first day.

1

Check eligibility

B.E / B.Tech: pass in 12th (HSC) with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, with the minimum marks set by AICTE / TNEA.

M.E / MBA / MCA: a relevant degree, plus a TANCET / CEETA-PG score.

2

Keep documents ready

  • 10th mark sheet
  • 12th mark sheet
  • Transfer certificate
  • Community certificate
  • Nativity / first-graduate certificate (if any)
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photos
  • Entrance score card (PG)
3

Choose us in TNEA

During TNEA counselling, use college code 1412.

4

Apply on the official site

Admissions run only on the college's official website, not on this project page.
